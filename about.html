Our history

Three decades of capsule making

  1. 1995Founded

    Honghui Xu founded Zhejiang Xinchang Honghui Capsule Factory in Xinchang, Zhejiang.

  2. 1998Production begins

    We obtained our capsule production licence and began full-scale production.

  3. 2001New home

    We moved to 788 Xinchang Avenue East Road and became Xinchang Honghui Capsule Co., Ltd.

  4. 2002Enteric capsules

    We developed and registered gastric-resistant and enteric capsules.

  5. 2003Expansion

    We expanded production and capital and became Zhejiang Xinchang Honghui Capsule Co., Ltd.

  6. 2007Zhejiang Famous Brand

    Our capsules were named a Zhejiang Famous Brand Product.

  7. 2009Famous Trademark

    Our trademark was recognised as a Zhejiang Famous Trademark.

  8. 2012Quality first

    When a national quality crackdown closed many capsule makers, our strict raw-material and process standards kept us running and earned lasting customer trust.

  9. 2014HPMC capsules

    Under a technology agreement with the Hangzhou Tea Research Institute of China Co-op, we developed plant-based HPMC capsules.

  10. 2015Pullulan and export

    We developed pullulan capsules, entered the Russian market and became a National High-Tech Enterprise.

  11. 2017Research and the US

    We developed carrageenan capsules with the College of Chemical Engineering at Huaqiao University and grew our business in the US.

  12. TodayA global supplier

    20 production lines, about 28 billion capsules a year, USDA Organic pullulan, and warehouses in Los Angeles and Vancouver.
